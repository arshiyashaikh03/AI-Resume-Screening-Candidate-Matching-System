# AI Resume Screening & Candidate Analysis System

## Project Overview

The AI Resume Screening & Candidate Analysis System is an AI-powered recruitment support system designed to reduce the repetitive work involved in manually reviewing resumes.

The system takes candidate resumes in PDF format, extracts the resume information, analyzes it against a predefined Business Analyst job description, and converts the results into structured candidate data.

The system is designed as a **Human-in-the-Loop** solution. AI provides evidence-based candidate analysis, while the final recruitment decision remains with the human recruiter.

---

## Problem Statement

Recruiters may need to review a large number of resumes for a single job opening. Manually extracting skills, education, experience, and matching them against job requirements can be repetitive and time-consuming.

The goal of this project is to automate the repetitive analysis process while keeping the recruiter involved in the final decision.
