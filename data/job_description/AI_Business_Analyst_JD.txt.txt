# AI Business Analyst

## About the Role

We are looking for an AI Business Analyst who can understand business requirements, analyze data, work with AI tools, and identify opportunities for process improvement and automation.

The candidate will work between business teams and technology teams to understand problems, document requirements, analyze data, and support AI-powered solutions.

## Required Skills

### 1. SQL

Ability to query and analyze structured business data using SQL.

### 2. Microsoft Excel

Ability to clean, organize, analyze, and present business data using Excel.

### 3. Power BI

Ability to create dashboards, reports, KPIs, and business insights using Power BI.

### 4. Business Analysis

Understanding of business problems, requirements analysis, stakeholder needs, and process improvement.

### 5. Generative AI

Understanding of Generative AI concepts and practical business applications.

### 6. Prompt Engineering

Ability to design effective prompts for AI models to generate useful and structured outputs.

### 7. Communication

Ability to communicate business requirements, findings, and recommendations clearly.

### 8. Requirements Gathering

Ability to identify, document, and analyze business and functional requirements.

### 9. Process Mapping

Ability to document and understand current and future business processes.

## Preferred Skills

* n8n
* Zapier
* API integration
* JSON
* Python
* AI automation
* Data visualization

## Responsibilities

* Gather and document business requirements.
* Analyze business and operational data.
* Create dashboards and reports.
* Identify process improvement opportunities.
* Support AI and automation initiatives.
* Design and test AI-assisted workflows.
* Communicate insights to business and technical stakeholders.
* Document requirements, workflows, and process changes.
* Assist teams in evaluating AI-powered business solutions.

## Education

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Administration, or a related field.

## Experience

Internship or project experience in business analysis, data analytics, AI, automation, or related areas is preferred.
